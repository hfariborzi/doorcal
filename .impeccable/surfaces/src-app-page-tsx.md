# Surface brief: landing page (midore.app /)

Scope: the public landing page, `src/app/page.tsx` and `src/components/landing/`. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: busy people with full calendars, juggling work and the rest of life. Action: sign in with Google (Microsoft when enabled). Proof: the real mechanism shown working (meetings fixed, life fitted into gaps), Dori, the trust facts in PRODUCT.md. No pricing, no invented customers.
Memorable moment: dragging a magnet across the week; it snaps into a free cell and refuses to sit on a meeting.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a fridge-door week planner: a white enamel board where meetings are printed on and everything else in life arrives as magnets that snap into the gaps. It refuses the category default of a tilted app screenshot over gradient blobs followed by a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Enamel-white board with a printed cool-grey half-hour grid inside a brushed aluminium frame; headlines in heavy marker-black; magnets are thick glossy strips with a lip shadow: vermilion for work, amber for life, sage for people, fixed meetings printed flat in light grey ink with charcoal text, no gloss (changed from charcoal fill at build: a dark print outweighed the magnets, and the magnets must lead); round dot magnets; dry-erase handwriting for notes; a metal tray ledge holding spare magnets and Dori.

STORY: The visitor sees a whole week (work, friends, gym, chores, a lazy Sunday), understands that meetings stay put and life is fitted around them, believes Midore is calm and personal rather than another work tool, and signs in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One framed board fills the viewport. Left 40%: the midore magnet logo, headline at display size, one line of subcopy, the Google sign-in as the primary action, three small checks. Right 60%: Mon–Sun grid; meetings already printed; magnets snap in on load in registration order; Sunday holds "Nothing planned. Chill?" in marker. The tray runs along the bottom with Dori and loose magnets.

FORM: Magnetic weekly planner board, position 6 of the ordered list. seed: none (the roll ran and assigned position 6 but printed no seed reference). Signature interaction: drag any life magnet; it snaps to whole half-hour cells and springs back off a printed meeting. Motion grammar: snap with one damped overshoot, staged in print order; nothing scattered.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
